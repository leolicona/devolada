## Summary

The visual execution of both features is strong: the "Adeudo anterior" line
lands consistently on all four money surfaces, the `partial` state speaks in
pesos with no percentage and no false green tick, and `withheld` gets its own
amber "Sin reactivar" badge instead of a lying red one. The craft the charge
path established carries over.

The biggest finding is not visual: **the pago page still refuses the short
receipt that PR #83 exists to accept**. The client-side amount gate predates
the feature and refuses any mismatch, so through the primary door (upload a
receipt) a short payer hits a dead end that tells them to call their ISP —
while the API, three tests deep, proudly records the same payment as
`partial`. The two test suites now assert opposite contracts and both pass.

The second theme is arithmetic honesty in the admin feed: a partial charge's
expanded row derives a "Total $514.00" from its breakdown lines while the row
itself says $300.00 — two totals, same card, no explanation, on the screen
whose brief principle is "never show a number that can't be explained".

## Must Fix

1. **A short receipt is refused by the UI that the whole feature was built to accept.**
   `apps/pago/src/features/pago/PaymentPage.tsx:389` (silent-path short-circuit)
   and `:713` (draft screen) gate on `readAmount !== data.totalCents`, but the
   spec's contract is directional: *"A receipt claiming less is now a valid
   submission; a receipt claiming more stays a mismatch"* (partial-payment
   D1/D12). The apiCEP-filter rationale in the code comment is obsolete — D12
   now sends the receipt's own amount to Banxico (`claimed_amount_cents`). The
   payer who transferred $499 of $664 uploads their receipt and gets "no
   podemos verificarlo… contacta a tu proveedor" — the exact six-hour-silence
   class of failure this spec set out to remove, now instant. Meanwhile
   `apps/api/test/direct-payment.test.ts:1074` asserts a $1 receipt becomes a
   $1 partial payment and `apps/pago/test/pago.test.tsx:435` asserts the same
   receipt is refused with zero submissions; both pass. See
   `review-pago-recibo-corto-rechazado-375.png`. _Fix: refuse only
   `readAmount > totalCents` (a misread, per the contract); submit short
   readings with `receiptAmountCents`; rewrite pago scenario 57 to the new
   contract and add the missing scenario-9 UI test (the spec's DoD already
   lists it unchecked). The "contacta a tu proveedor" copy then only ever
   faces over-claims, where it is right._

2. **The feed shows two disagreeing totals for a partial charge.**
   `AmountBreakdown` derives its total by summing its lines
   (`packages/ui/src/components/amount-breakdown.tsx:40`), and
   `FeedScreen.tsx:92-102` feeds it the *ask* (invoice + fee) while the row
   header shows the *received* (`totalCents`). A withheld $300 payment expands
   into "Mensualidad $499.00 · Cargo por servicio $15.00 · **Total $514.00**"
   under a row that says **$300.00**, with nothing naming the $214 gap — see
   `review-admin-cobros-parcial-1280.png`. Brief principle 3: every number
   must be explainable by tapping it; this tap creates the question. The
   spec's contract line — *a partial payment appears with its own label,
   "pago parcial"* — is also unimplemented (the row is distinguishable only
   by its amber badge). _Fix: when `totalCents` < the ask, render the ask
   breakdown plus explicit "Recibido" / "Pendiente" lines (needs a small spec
   decision on the exact shape — the fields are all on `feedCharge`), and the
   "pago parcial" label beside the channel line._

3. **"Ver los datos para transferir" does not show the data for transferring.**
   The partial state's one action (`PaymentPage.tsx:587`) calls `retry()`,
   which clears the payment but leaves the remembered step at `proof` — so the
   button lands on "Envía tu comprobante" (upload form, empty manual fields),
   with the CLABE still one more tap away behind "Ver los datos otra vez".
   Compare `review-pago-parcial-375.png` with where it lands,
   `review-pago-parcial-regreso-375.png`. The spec's UI contract goes further
   than the button: *"The page keeps the SPEI instructions visible in the
   `partial` state… making them navigate back to find the CLABE is a way to
   lose them."* The shipped page is two taps and one mislabel away from the
   CLABE. _Fix: at minimum `goTo("transfer")` inside the partial retry;
   better, render the SPEI card inline under the missing amount as the spec
   asks — and if one-tap-away is the deliberate choice, the spec must say so
   (the golden rule forbids the fork either way)._

## Should Fix

4. **"Mensualidad" now labels a number that is not the mensualidad.** After
   D8, `invoiceCents` is the invoice total — proration, discounts, the
   reconnection charge that started this work. D13 renamed the wire field for
   exactly that reason, quoting the failure: *"it would read 'Mensualidad
   $649' on a receipt whose $150 is a reconnection charge."* The wire was
   fixed; the word survived on all four surfaces
   (`ConfirmScreen.tsx:143`, `FeedScreen.tsx:94`, `PaymentPage.tsx:802`,
   `apps/api/src/receipt/index.ts:29`). On the demo tenant invoice ==
   plan price, which is why nobody sees it — D8 calls that invisibility out
   as the trap. _Fix: a glossary decision first (one word per concept), then
   the label — "Cargo del periodo" or similar — applied everywhere at once._

5. **The partial state's copy and polling assume `withheld`.**
   `PaymentPage.tsx:569-571` says "se reactivará cuando llegue el resto" for
   any non-reconnected status, and the poll stops for `partial`
   (`PaymentPage.tsx:299` keeps only `validating`/`confirmed` open). A partial
   that met the threshold but queued its reconnection (WispHub down — the
   product's founding failure mode) is told the rest is needed when it is
   not, and the page never updates to "ya está activo". _Fix: keep polling
   `partial` while `reconnectionStatus === "queued"`; branch the sentence on
   `withheld` vs `queued`/`failed`._

6. **A withheld charge is invisible to the feed's filters.** The chips are
   Todos / En cola / Fallidos / Reconectados (`FeedScreen.tsx:29-34`); the API
   already accepts `status=withheld` (`charges/schema.ts:69`). Money that
   arrived without reactivating anyone is precisely what an ISP wants to
   audit, and the only way to see it is to scroll "Todos". _Fix: a "Sin
   reactivar" chip._

7. **The browser layer tests a wire shape that no longer exists.**
   `tests/e2e/stubs.ts` still sends `monthlyFeeCents` (quote, customers, feed,
   paymentLink) and no `channel`; the screens read `invoiceCents`/
   `carriedBalanceCents`, and `formatMoney(undefined)` renders **$NaN**
   without throwing — so `pnpm e2e` and the screenshot harness pass while
   showing fiction. This is the previous review's "harness gap" lesson
   recurring one layer down: the assertions check geometry, and geometry
   survives NaN. _Fix: update the stubs to the #82 shape; type them against
   the real contracts (`z.infer` of `quoteResponse`, `feedResponse`, …) so
   the next rename fails at `pnpm typecheck` instead of silently. An
   `expect(page.getByText("NaN")).toHaveCount(0)` guard in the e2e base would
   close the class._

8. **The ISP cannot set the two numbers that govern all of this.**
   `SettingsScreen.tsx` has no `reconnectionThresholdPercent` /
   `reconnectionFloorCents` controls — tracked, unchecked, in the spec's DoD,
   so this is a known gap rather than news. The product consequence is worth
   stating: every ISP sits at 100/$0, and D3's own arithmetic shows the
   default *refuses the founding scenario* ($499 of $649 = 77%). Until
   Configuración ships, the feature records short payments but no ISP can
   choose to reconnect anyone on one. _Fix: build the DoD item — two fields
   plus the one-line explanation of the default the spec asks for._

## Could Improve

9. **"Faltan $150.00" is the payer's one actionable number, set in `text-sm`.**
   The brief's aesthetic is protagonist amounts; on this screen the
   protagonist is the badge, and the number to transfer is a body-text line
   (`review-pago-parcial-375.png`). _Suggestion: give the missing amount the
   amount treatment — larger, semibold, `tabular-nums` — above the folio._

10. **The search amount and the confirm total differ by the fee with no cue.**
    Janely reads $649.00 in results and $664.00 on confirm
    (`review-tienda-buscar-adeudo-375.png` vs `-confirmar-`). Pre-existing
    pattern, but #82 turned the search number into "the debt", making the gap
    read as an error. _Suggestion: label it — "Debe $649.00" — or show the
    customer-facing total in results._

11. **The withheld receipt names the remainder without its amount.**
    `receipt/index.ts:19` says "cuando llegue el resto del adeudo"; D7's
    principle is that the missing number **is** the instruction, and the
    charge row carries enough to compute it. _Suggestion: append "faltan
    $X" when the fields allow it._

## What Works Well

- **The `partial` state is D7 rendered faithfully**: three amounts in pesos,
  no percentage anywhere, no green over a cut service, folio for the support
  call it should prevent. The dark variant is a real palette, not an
  inversion (`review-pago-parcial-375-dark.png`).
- **"Adeudo anterior" is one decision executed four times identically** —
  confirm screen, feed detail, payment page, receipt — conditional exactly
  when nonzero, so the common case stays two lines. The confirm screen shows
  the unfamiliar total *with* its explanation, which is precisely the
  shopkeeper-freezes scenario D11 was written against
  (`review-tienda-confirmar-adeudo-375.png`).
- **`withheld` refuses to lie in either direction**: amber, PauseCircle, "Sin
  reactivar" — not the red "Fallido" that sends the ISP hunting a bug, not
  the green that tells a cut customer they're fine. Colour never travels
  alone, per the law.
- **Search now shows what is owed, not the list price** — the invisible-debtor
  case (carried balance, empty invoice list) is findable at the counter.
- **The spec-first discipline shows.** Every UI conditional in the diff cites
  its decision; reviewing this was reading an argument, not reconstructing
  one. The findings above are almost all places where the *last mile* of a
  decision (a label, a filter, a stub, a client gate) lagged the wire — worth
  a standing checklist item: when a wire shape changes, grep the stubs and
  the words, not just the fields.
